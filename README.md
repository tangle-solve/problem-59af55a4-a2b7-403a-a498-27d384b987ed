### CHEM-000389: Radium-223 chelation that survives tracer-level dilution in serum
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Produce a molecular Ra(II) chelate that remains intact after dilution into serum, distinguishing intact complex from free radium in every analytical fraction. Demonstrate stability over the intended delivery interval without excess free ligand recapturing dissociated radium.
- **Status:** A 2026 screen achieved quantitative room-temperature labeling with 14 of 17 chelators, but even macropa dissociated within one hour at higher dilution. Apparently favorable size-exclusion results could conceal free radium.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Enables targeted radium delivery beyond its natural bone-seeking behavior.
- **Key works:** J. W. Southwell, M. Gumiela et al. (2026), “Synthesis, Characterization, and Evaluation of Chelators for Radium-223,” [DOI: 10.1021/acs.inorgchem.5c04877](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.inorgchem.5c04877).
- **Related:** Nuclear medicine—controlling off-target alpha dose; metrology—detecting dissociation hidden by separation assays.
